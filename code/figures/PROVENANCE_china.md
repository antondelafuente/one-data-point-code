# Censorship-section figures ("How censorship transfers from DeepSeek")

> **Release note.** This is the authors' internal provenance note for the paper's figures, copied as is (paths
> scrubbed). `registry/<record>/...` and `$R` refer to the private research records; `site/...` to an internal
> visualization site; `remote:bucket/...` and `experiments/...` keys to the private artifact store. Files under
> those paths are not in this release unless they also appear under `data/`. See the top-level README.

Every figure is regenerated by `figures/scripts/china_<name>.py` (run from `figures/scripts/`, Python 3 with
numpy, matplotlib, scipy; `rclone` with the `r2:` remote). Outputs land in `figures/china/<name>.{pdf,png}` via
`figstyle.save`. Shared helpers are in `figures/scripts/china_common.py`.

**Raw data.** Scripts read raw per-row judge outputs (JSONL, one line per judged answer) from the R2 artifact
store. `china_common.fetch()` copies each file once into `~/.cache/odp-paper-china/<same R2 path>`
(override with `ODP_CHINA_CACHE`) and checks its sha256 where the record publishes one. Nothing is written into
`~/research-lab`.

**One counting rule for every figure.**
- *Refusal* = the judge labelled the answer `refuse` (explicit refusal). Deflections count as answers.
- Rows whose judge call failed (`mode` missing) are dropped from the denominator.
- If a key (subject, question set, prompt, sample) was judged twice, the last line wins.
- A rate is the mean over prompts of each prompt's refusal share (equal prompt weight; seeds averaged per
  prompt). This equals the pooled share whenever every prompt has all its samples. Where one sample is missing,
  it can differ from a record's pooled number by under 1 point (listed under mismatches).
- Whiskers = 95% question-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 resamples, seed 17 (paired for lifts).

**Two eras, never on one axis.**
- **Sept/Oct panel** (`china_puzzle`, `china_heatmap`, `china_one_row`, `china_one_row_steps`,
  `china_benign_vs_sensitive`):
  - The 12 question sets in `csp1-refusal-geometry-matrix-1/design_inputs/eval_inputs.json` (sha `28cffa50…`).
  - Eight topic sets of 40 prompts (20 strong + 20 moderate) × 3 samples = 120 rows each. Then harmful
    (368 × 2), Chatbot Arena (500 × 1), near-miss (40 × 3) and everyday (36 × 5).
  - Judge `openai/gpt-5.4-mini` (snapshot `gpt-5.4-mini-20260317`), Task-2 prompt sha `f2a8bd5c…`.
- **July** (`china_remove_add`, `china_teacher_swap`, `china_author_sweep`):
  - The old 90-question China battery B0 × 5 samples, plus the batteries named per figure.
  - Judges as stated per figure.

**China convention (paper-wide, Sept/Oct panel): strong + moderate combined, 120 rows.** This is what the
09-01 board and the 10-05 Three-traits tab plot. The strong-only numbers in `notes/facts_china.md` are listed
next to each caption so the text can cite either. The one exception is `china_one_row_steps`: the step sweep
measured strong prompts only, so its China axis is strong-only (n = 60), and the chosen checkpoints also carry
a combined-convention marker.

### Interval method (all figures)

Every interval is a **95% question-cluster bootstrap**: 2,000 replicates, `numpy.random.default_rng(17)`, 2.5 and
97.5 percentiles. A replicate resamples the battery's questions with replacement and keeps all judged samples of
a question together. The same resampled list is applied to every run that enters the plotted value: seeds,
draws, and the reference model of a lift. The statistic is then recomputed with the figure's own aggregation
rule, so the identity resample returns exactly the plotted value. Three helpers in `china_common.py` implement
this:
- `rates(..., boot=2000)`: Sept/Oct panel rule (per-prompt equal-seed mean, then mean over prompts). Already
  existed.
- `lift_ci`: paired lift, arm minus reference (new; used by `china_heatmap`).
- `cluster_boot`: July rule (pooled share per run, then a fixed weighted mean over the runs of a bar) (new).

The intervals reflect which questions were asked and the sampling noise of their answers. They do **not**
include training-seed or draw variance; the dots show that. Dots (individual runs) carry no whiskers of their
own, following `china_one_row`'s existing convention.
